Charity Relief Project

Bringing direct financial relief to those who need it most

Our Mission

Our core mission is to find and gain financial support to offer direct cash assistance to vulnerable individuals in our community.

Who We Help

We focus on supporting people who are deeply struggling with their lives, specifically:

  • Older individuals facing severe hardships.
  • Low-income individuals and families.
  • People suffering from long-term, chronic diseases.
